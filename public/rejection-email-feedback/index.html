How it works

  1. Paste the rejection email, or try one of the examples.
  2. Say how far you got and how long after applying it arrived.
  3. Read the likely reasons and next steps, then send the reply if it fits.

What you get

  • What the email does and doesn't tell you
  • Likely reasons for a rejection after applying, a phone screen, interviews or a final round
  • What to change before your next application
  • A short, gracious reply you can send

Questions

Can it tell me the real reason I was rejected?

No. Rejection emails rarely give the real reason. This gives a best guess based on the stage and the wording, not a verdict.

Should I reply to a rejection email?

If you met people in interviews, a short thank-you that asks to be considered for future roles is worth sending. For an automatic email after applying, it usually isn't needed.

Is the email uploaded?

No. It's read in your browser.

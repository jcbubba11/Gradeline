How it works

  1. Drop in your resume (PDF, Word or text), or paste it.
  2. Optionally paste the job posting and pick your field.
  3. Read the score, then accept or skip each red-pen suggestion.

What it checks

  • Contact details, standard sections and dates on every role
  • Bullets that start with an action verb and include a number, % or $
  • Duty language like "responsible for" and unproven buzzwords like "team player"
  • Length (about one to two pages) and bullet length
  • Personal details to leave off, such as birth date, marital status or "references available"
  • Keywords and the job title from the posting you paste
  • Extra checks for nursing, tech, teaching, accounting, creative and skilled-trades resumes

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The grader, builder, templates and downloads are free, with no account and no paywall.

Is my resume uploaded?

No. Grading runs in your browser and your resume stays on your device. The only time anything is saved online is if you choose to create a Mentor review link to share.

Where do the rules come from?

Each check links the career-center or hiring guidance it is based on, from sources such as Harvard, MIT and Purdue career offices. The How we grade page lists them all.

Can a score guarantee an interview?

No. It is an automated first pass. It can't judge truthfulness, design or fit for a role, so have a person read your resume too.

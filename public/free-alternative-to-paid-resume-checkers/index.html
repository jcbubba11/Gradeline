Questions

What's the catch?

There isn't one for the core tools. Gradeline doesn't sell your data or need an account.

Does Gradeline use AI to rewrite my resume?

No. The checks are transparent rules with sources. Any suggestion is shown to you first, and nothing changes unless you accept it.

Is it as good as a paid tool?

It covers the checks that career centers recommend most. Paid tools may add features like AI rewriting. Try both and keep whichever helps you more.

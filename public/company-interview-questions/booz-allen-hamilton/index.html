How the interview usually goes

  1. Online application
  2. Recruiter screen
  3. Behavioral and technical interviews; many roles require clearance eligibility

What Booz Allen Hamilton looks for

Booz Allen works mostly for government clients. Clearance eligibility and mission focus come up.

Questions to prepare

  1. Are you eligible for a security clearance?
  2. Why Booz Allen?
  3. Tell me about a time you worked on a mission bigger than yourself.
  4. Tell me about a time you solved a technical problem for a client.

Based on what Booz Allen Hamilton publishes about its hiring and on commonly reported questions. Steps and questions vary by team, role and year, so ask your recruiter what to expect. That's a normal question. One Stop isn't affiliated with Booz Allen Hamilton.

How to prepare

  1. Answer behavioral questions with STAR: situation, task, action, result. Spend most of your time on what you did and what happened.
  2. Prepare six to eight stories you can adapt, and one for each of the values Booz Allen Hamilton talks about.
  3. Read the job posting and your resume again the day before. Every bullet is a claim they may ask about.
  4. Have two or three questions ready to ask them about the team and the role.

Free ATS resume checker

Applicant tracking systems read your resume as plain text before a person does. Gradeline's "What the robot sees" view shows you that text, so you can catch columns, tables and headers that come out scrambled.

How it works

  1. Open What the robot sees.
  2. Drop in your resume PDF or Word file. It's read in your browser, never uploaded.
  3. Fix anything that came through out of order or missing, then send the text to the grader.

What it checks

  • The plain text an ATS would pull from your file, line by line
  • What it recognized, such as your name, contact details and sections
  • Layout warnings for things like merged columns, icons that turn into junk characters and contact details hidden in a page header
  • Single-column templates and a builder that export ATS-readable PDF and Word files

Questions

Is this the same as a real ATS?

No tool can copy every ATS exactly. This shows the plain-text reading most of them start from, which is where most formatting problems show up.

Do I need a special ATS template?

A clean single-column layout with standard headings works best. All of Gradeline's templates and builder layouts follow that.

Questions

What is the STAR method?

STAR stands for situation, task, action and result. It's a simple way to answer "Tell me about a time..." questions: a sentence or two of background, then most of your time on what you did and what happened as a result.

How long should an interview answer be?

About one to two minutes for most behavioral answers. The practice timer is set to two minutes so you can get a feel for it.

Are my answers saved?

Yes, in your browser on this device. They aren't uploaded. If you sign in, they sync to your account so you can see them on another device.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no paywall.

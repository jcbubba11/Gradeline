Job application kit

One job, everything you need to apply. Paste the posting and your resume. You'll get a resume check against that posting with the keywords you're missing, a cover letter draft built from your strongest bullets, a short LinkedIn note for the recruiter, and a follow-up plan with dates.

Build my kit

How it works

  1. Paste the job posting, or load a job you saved from Find jobs.
  2. Load your resume from the grader or the builder.
  3. Work through the kit: fix your resume, finish the cover letter, then send the note and follow up on schedule.

What's in the kit

  • A resume score for this posting, with the keywords it uses that your resume doesn't
  • Each requirement in the posting matched to the line on your resume that proves it
  • A cover letter draft built from your own bullets, with [brackets] where you add your own words
  • A short LinkedIn connection note for the recruiter or hiring manager
  • A follow-up plan you can add to your job tracker

Questions

Does it write my cover letter for me?

It drafts one from your own resume bullets so you aren't starting from a blank page. You fill in the bracketed parts and edit it until it sounds like you.

Is the posting or my resume uploaded?

No. The kit is worked out in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account and no paywall.

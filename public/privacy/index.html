Privacy

Your resume stays on your device. One Stop reads your files and grades them inside your browser, so we never receive them. Accounts are optional. Effective November 4, 2026.

The short version

  • Files you drop in are read by your browser. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Your work is saved in your browser's storage on your device, not on a server, unless you sign in.
  • Accounts are optional. If you don't sign in, we don't collect your name, email or resume.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and we don't sell data.
  • We count visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or collect personal information, so we can see which tools people use.

What is stored, and where

In your browser only. The resume or letter you're grading, job postings you paste, builder content, LinkedIn checker entries and post drafts, application kits, follow-up messages, tracker rows, saved jobs, interview answers, your chosen field and checklist ticks. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them, and so does the button at the bottom of this page.

If you create an account (optional). Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores your name, email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your profile picture. While you're signed in, the same work listed above is copied to this site's database (Google Cloud Firestore) under your account, so it syncs across your devices. Security rules let only you read it.

Job alerts (only if you turn them on). Alerts need an account with a confirmed email. We store your email address, your saved searches and when we last emailed you. About once a day, a scheduled job compares new listings with your searches and sends one digest email through Gmail. Turn alerts off on Find jobs to stop them.

Job tracker reminders. Follow-up reminders are worked out in your browser. If you download a calendar file, it goes to your device and we never see it.

Mentor review (only if you use it). When you create a review link, the document text, your name and note, and reviewers' names and comments are saved in the site's database so people with the link can see them. Links are random and not listed anywhere, but anyone who has one can read and comment, so think about removing your phone number and address first.

Backups. "Download backup" saves a file to your device. We never see it.

Usage stats

To learn which tools are useful, we count page views with GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly analytics service. It records the page or tool opened (for example "Interview prep"), the site that linked you here, and your browser, operating system, screen size and country. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't store your IP address, and doesn't follow you to other sites. It never sees your resume or anything you type.

Review links are counted as "review" without their ID. Visits aren't counted if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or if you switch counting off here:

See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Job listings

Find jobs shows openings collected from employers' public career pages several times a day. Searching and filtering happen in your browser. When you apply, you go to the employer's own site, and their privacy policy applies there.

Services the site loads

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries (jsPDF and pdf.js) from cdnjs, run by Cloudflare. Signing in, job alerts and Mentor review load Google's Firebase library from jsDelivr. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website request, these services receive your IP address and browser details. They don't receive your resume. See the privacy policies of Google, Cloudflare and GitHub.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all my data in this browser" on the in-app privacy page to clear everything stored on this device. If you have an account, sign in first and remove your work, turn off alerts, then contact us to delete the account itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
